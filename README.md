# Furniture Store-sql-analysis
SQL analysis on store dataset to derive business trends and insights

#Business problem:
Analyze the Store dataset to identify high-performing products, customer segments, regional sales trends, and year-over-year growth to support better business decision-making.

#Key Findings:
--Chairs dominate revenue, followed by Tables, making them the primary sales drivers.
--Revenue shows consistent growth (2014–2017), with a strong surge after 2015, indicating business expansion.
--Consumer segment contributes the majority (53%), with Corporate as the next key segment.
--Profitability is impacted by discounting, highlighting the need for a better pricing and discount strategy.
--California generates the highest sales among all states

#Tools used:
Postgre sql. Google colab . Pandas. Tableau. Excel
